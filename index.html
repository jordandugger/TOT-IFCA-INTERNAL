Step 2 You probably have questions — here are honest answers to everything you might be wondering

Your Questions. Answered.

We anticipated what you'd want to know. Here are honest answers to everything.
What is actually happening — is IFCA being sold? ▼
Yes. IFCA has been acquired by Taeler De Haes, the founder of Fitness Coach Academy (FCA). This was a deliberate, multi-year process — not a rushed decision. Jordan and Erin spent a long time finding the right person to carry IFCA forward. Taeler is that person. The full story is in the announcement video and the podcast below.
Are Jordan and Erin still involved? ▼
Yes — through a 90-day active transition period running through the end of Q3 2026. During that window, Jordan and Erin are engaged daily: on calls, in the community, available. After Q3, Taeler leads fully and Jordan and Erin step into a lighter advisory role. They will not disappear on day one. The podcast below has Jordan and Erin walking through their reasoning in full.
Will my success coach change? ▼
No. Your current success coach is staying. The entire IFCA coaching team remains in place through and beyond the transition. Kevin Hamidi continues as Head Success Coach. The individual relationship you have with your coach is not being disrupted. Nothing in your day-to-day coaching experience changes on day one.
What happens to my contract and payment plan? ▼
Every contract and payment plan is fully honored. This is a legal condition of the acquisition — your enrollment terms are protected exactly as written when you signed. No changes to your billing schedule, amounts, or any terms of your enrollment. If you want your specific contract confirmed in writing, reply to the student email and our team will send it to you directly.
Will the curriculum change? ▼
The core IFCA Accelerator curriculum is not changing. The system you enrolled in is the system you're going to complete. What Taeler is adding over time: her FCA systems and content will be integrated to strengthen what's already there. We add — we don't subtract. Taeler's commitments on curriculum integrity are covered in detail in the podcast below.
Who is Taeler De Haes? ▼
Taeler is the founder of Fitness Coach Academy (FCA), where she built a community of coaches to consistent 5- and 6-figure months. She started with $825 in her bank account and generated $250,000 in her first year as an online coach — using the same systems IFCA teaches. She's not a corporate buyer. She's a coach who built exactly what you're building. That's why Jordan and Erin chose her over every other candidate.
Why did Jordan and Erin decide to sell? ▼
This wasn't burnout and it wasn't a reactive decision. IFCA reaching its next level of growth requires someone whose only full focus is this company. Jordan and Erin built IFCA to outlast them — that was always the plan. They found the person who can take this further than they could by splitting focus. The podcast below is the honest, unscripted version of how that decision was made.
What's the biggest change I'll actually notice? ▼
New energy and a new face leading the company. Taeler is a hands-on operator — she'll be present in the community and on calls in ways that founders splitting time across two companies and two families simply can't be. FCA students are also joining the IFCA ecosystem, which expands the peer community around you. The day-to-day experience gets fuller, not thinner.
Is IFCA going to become more corporate or lose its culture? ▼
No. Taeler is a solo founder who built FCA the same way Jordan and Erin built IFCA — close to her clients and community. She's not a PE-backed buyer. She's not building a brand roll-up. The core values of IFCA — Relentless Growth, Extreme Ownership, Authentic Connection — those are the standard Taeler is operating from. That's not a talking point. It's what she was vetted against.
What is FCA and what does it mean that they're merging? ▼
FCA (Fitness Coach Academy) is Taeler's coaching education company. It's being phased out as a standalone offer over the coming months, and FCA students are transitioning into the IFCA ecosystem. For IFCA students, this means a richer peer community, more resources, and a more focused operator at the top. Two communities, one direction — with IFCA as the platform.
Will the GHL tools and software access change? ▼
No. GHL is a core part of the IFCA toolkit and it's staying. Your subscription and access remain as-is. If anything, the tech infrastructure is getting stronger — Cory Carpenter is actively developing new AI tools inside the IFCA ecosystem post-acquisition.
I have a question that wasn't answered here — who do I ask? ▼
Reply directly to the student email that brought you here. Jordan or a member of our team will respond personally. We're also collecting real questions from the community to address in the May 21 FAQ email — so if something is on your mind, send it. There are no bad questions right now.
Why are Jordan and Erin selling instead of just hiring more help? ▼
Hiring help and transferring ownership solve different problems. IFCA at its next level needs a founder-level operator — someone with full ownership, full accountability, and full focus. A hire doesn't create that. What Taeler brings isn't a resume. It's a decade of building in this exact space, an entire team behind her, and the kind of hunger that only comes with skin in the game. Jordan and Erin built IFCA to outlast them. This is what that looks like in practice.
What does Taeler's team bring that IFCA didn't already have? ▼
Three things primarily: organic client acquisition systems, a high-performing sales team with a proven track record on cold traffic, and a community-first culture that Taeler has built from the ground up at FCA. IFCA's strength has always been curriculum depth, infrastructure, and back-end delivery. FCA's strength is top-of-funnel growth, DM-based acquisition, and founder-level client relationships. Combined, that's a more complete machine — and your business benefits from all of it.
Will prices change for current students or when I go to renew? ▼
Your current enrollment terms don't change — full stop. For renewals and future enrollment, offer pricing will be set by Taeler and the new IFCA leadership team. What we can tell you is that as the program gets stronger and more resourced, the pricing will reflect that value. Nobody currently enrolled is being renegotiated mid-contract.
What happens to the Legacy Mastermind and 7-Figure CEO containers? ▼
Both containers are continuing. Legacy Mastermind is being rebranded to Path to 4% on July 1 — the content, format, and team behind it are not changing. 7-Figure CEO remains. Cory Carpenter, who built both containers, remains the operational lead on high-level delivery. If you're currently enrolled in either, your access and terms are fully protected.
How was Taeler chosen? Was there a competitive process? ▼
Yes. Jordan and Erin evaluated multiple buyers — people inside and outside the fitness industry, with different operational backgrounds and different visions for IFCA. The criteria were non-negotiable: protect the culture, honor the students, bring more than capital, and have the operator profile to take IFCA to $10M+ annually. Taeler was the only candidate who checked every box. She was also the only candidate who had lived this industry from the inside, not as an investor looking at a return.
I'm currently in a back-end container (Legacy / 7-Figure CEO) — does anything change for me specifically? ▼
Nothing changes in your current container. Cory Carpenter is staying as the operator behind high-level delivery. Your contract, your access, your coaching team — all protected. The one thing you'll notice over time: Taeler is stepping into a more active role in high-level curriculum alongside Cory, which means more combined expertise going into those containers, not less.
Can I get to know Taeler before she officially takes over? ▼
Yes — in two ways. First, watch the announcement video and podcast on this page. Taeler is in both, unscripted, talking directly about what she's building and why. Second, Taeler is doing a live training inside the IFCA community in early June — a real State of the Union where you can ask her anything directly. She's also co-hosting a live masterclass with Jordan on June 11 at 7pm ET. Between now and then, you'll get a real read on who she is and how she operates.
Why is this announcement coming to me before it's public? ▼
Because you earned it. You're an active IFCA student — you're in the inner circle. The public announcement goes out May 18. We wanted you to hear this directly from us, with full context and a chance to ask questions, before it hits social media. Your success coach has also been briefed and is available if you want to talk through anything one-on-one.
