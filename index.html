Your Questions. Answered.

We anticipated what you'd want to know. Here are honest answers to everything.
What is actually happening — is IFCA being sold?▼
Yes. IFCA has been acquired by Taeler De Haes, the founder of Fitness Coach Academy (FCA). This was a deliberate, multi-year process — not a rushed decision. Jordan and Erin spent a long time finding the right person to carry IFCA forward. Taeler is that person. The full story is in the announcement video and the podcast below.
Are Jordan and Erin still involved?▼
Yes — through a 90-day active transition period running through the end of Q3 2026. During that window, Jordan and Erin are engaged daily: on calls, in the community, available. After Q3, Taeler leads fully and Jordan and Erin step into a lighter advisory role. They will not disappear on day one.
Will my success coach change?▼
No. Your current success coach is staying. The entire IFCA coaching team remains in place through and beyond the transition. Kevin Hamidi continues as Head Success Coach. The individual relationship you have with your coach is not being disrupted.
What happens to my contract and payment plan?▼
Every contract and payment plan is fully honored. This is a legal condition of the acquisition — your enrollment terms are protected exactly as written when you signed. No changes to your billing schedule, amounts, or any terms of your enrollment. If you want your specific contract confirmed in writing, reply to the student email and our team will send it to you directly.
Will the curriculum change?▼
Core curriculum stays. Taeler has helped countless coaches scale to $10k, $50k and $100k months in recurring revenue through organic-first strategies, and she'll be sharing all of her secrets, playbooks and strategies.
Who is Taeler De Haes?▼
Taeler started her online fitness coaching business in 2019 when she was laid off from her decade-long TV news career. With an Emmy in hand, and after 7 years of bodybuilding, she went from the "fit friend" to her first 7-figures in two years, without ever running an ad. She opened the doors to Fitness Coach Academy in 2021, a mentorship for coaches and personal trainers desiring to start and grow 6-figure online businesses. Since then, she's helped more than 1,500 coaches scale to $10k, $50k and $100k months in recurring revenue, without burnout, through organic-first strategies, elite coaching standards and sustainable business systems.
Why did Jordan and Erin decide to sell?▼
This wasn't burnout and it wasn't a reactive decision. IFCA reaching its next level of growth requires someone whose only full focus is this company. Jordan and Erin built IFCA to outlast them — that was always the plan. They found the person who can take this further than they could by splitting focus.
What's the biggest change I'll actually notice?▼
New energy and a new face leading the company. Taeler is a hands-on operator — she'll be present in the community and on calls in ways that founders splitting time across two companies and two families simply can't be. FCA students are also joining the IFCA ecosystem, which expands the peer community around you. The day-to-day experience gets fuller, not thinner.
Is IFCA going to become more corporate or lose its culture?▼
No. Taeler is a solo founder who built FCA the same way Jordan and Erin built IFCA — close to her clients and community. She's not a PE-backed buyer. She's not building a brand roll-up. The core values of IFCA — Relentless Growth, Extreme Ownership, Authentic Connection — those are the standard Taeler is operating from. That's not a talking point. It's what she was vetted against.
What is FCA and what does it mean that they're merging?▼
FCA (Fitness Coach Academy) is Taeler's coaching education company. It's being phased out as a standalone offer over the coming months, and FCA students are transitioning into the IFCA ecosystem. For IFCA students, this means a richer peer community, more resources, and a more focused operator at the top. Two communities, one direction — with IFCA as the platform.
Will the GHL tools and software access change?▼
No. GHL is a core part of the IFCA toolkit and it's staying. Your subscription and access remain as-is. If anything, the tech infrastructure is getting stronger — Cory Carpenter is actively developing new AI tools inside the IFCA ecosystem post-acquisition.
I have a question that wasn't answered here — who do I ask?▼
Reply directly to the student email that brought you here. Jordan or a member of our team will respond personally. We're also collecting real questions from the community to address in the May 21 FAQ email — so if something is on your mind, send it. There are no bad questions right now.
Why Taeler? How did you choose her?▼
Four reasons: legacy protection, client care, competitive drive and all-the-way in energy in the right season of life. Taeler's helped over 1,500 coaches, personal trainers and fitness professionals start and grow 7-figure businesses. She's also got skills we wish we had, and combining the two forces? You just got your new unfair advantage.
Will Taeler be accessible? Can we get to know her?▼
Absolutely! She'll be hosting a live training in the next few weeks for you to get to know her. In the meantime, follow her on Instagram (@taelerfit) to get to know her a little better.
Why is this announcement coming to me before it's public?▼
You're in the inner circle, and we wanted you to hear it from us first before it's public.
